Why the map screens looked like dots on black

The map shots in docs/screens.html showed the map tiles as a single foreground colour with no background — the generated tile sheets were being used as a colour source only, never as a picture. That was two bugs, and both are fixed:

  1. the tile art never painted. A map tile set one colour per cell, and that colour went into the glyph, so grass, water and stone were dots on black. Every tile picture now carries two colours — the tile's own colour, which becomes the background of the cell, and a shade for its texture glyph — and the blitter sets the cell attribute to (base << 4) | glyph.
  2. the screenshot renderer threw the background away. It skipped blank cells before painting the colour behind them, so even a frame the game had painted correctly came out black in the PNG. Fixed in tools/ptyrender.py.

The pictures below are real screens captured from the running binary in a pseudo terminal — the same frames that are embedded in docs/screens.html. A map tile can only ever be 2x2 characters (the view is 40 tiles wide on an 80-column terminal), which is why tiles carry a texture glyph rather than half-block pixels: at 2x4 pixels every tile collapses into a flat colour field.
